## 📋 Course Syllabus

### Course Overview
Develop essential aptitude and communication skills for technical interviews and competitive exams. Master quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and verbal ability.

### Topics Covered

#### 1. Quantitative Aptitude
- **Number Systems**
  - HCF and LCM
  - Prime numbers
  - Divisibility rules
- **Arithmetic**
  - Percentages and ratios
  - Profit and loss
  - Simple and compound interest
  - Time and work
  - Time, speed, and distance
- **Algebra**
  - Linear equations
  - Quadratic equations
  - Progressions (AP, GP)
- **Geometry**
  - Lines and angles
  - Triangles and circles
  - Area and perimeter
  - Volume and surface area
- **Data Interpretation**
  - Tables and charts
  - Graphs and diagrams
  - Data analysis

#### 2. Logical Reasoning
- **Verbal Reasoning**
  - Analogies and classifications
  - Series completion
  - Coding-decoding
  - Blood relations
  - Direction sense
- **Non-Verbal Reasoning**
  - Pattern recognition
  - Series and sequences
  - Mirror and water images
  - Paper folding and cutting
- **Analytical Reasoning**
  - Puzzles and arrangements
  - Syllogisms
  - Statements and conclusions
  - Cause and effect

#### 3. Verbal Ability
- **Grammar**
  - Parts of speech
  - Tenses and voice
  - Articles and prepositions
  - Subject-verb agreement
- **Vocabulary**
  - Synonyms and antonyms
  - One-word substitution
  - Idioms and phrases
- **Reading Comprehension**
  - Passage analysis
  - Inference and conclusion
  - Main idea identification
- **Sentence Correction**
  - Error spotting
  - Sentence improvement
  - Para jumbles

#### 4. Communication Skills
- Effective communication
- Presentation skills
- Group discussion techniques
- Interview preparation
- Email and business writing

### Learning Outcomes
- Solve quantitative problems efficiently
- Apply logical reasoning
- Improve verbal and written communication
- Excel in technical interviews
